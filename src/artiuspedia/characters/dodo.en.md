Lively and determined, Miss Dodo is one of the city’s sculptors. Popularly known as a nomad, she’s always wandering around Rupestrina in search of new inspirations. Her nephew Tyler is her creative partner and adventure companion. Her iconic works are scattered all over, eternalizing the history and events of the region!
