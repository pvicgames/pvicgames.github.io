Clumsy but incredibly stylish, Tyler is one of Rupestrina’s biggest film buffs. Although shy and awkward, he’s always around with his clapperboard, searching for everyday scenes that might inspire his next script. Tyler was born in a neighboring town but adopted Rupestrina as his home. Enchanted by the city, he quickly became involved in its cultural scene.
