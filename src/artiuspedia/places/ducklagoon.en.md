Nestled between water on all sides, Rupestrine is a secluded city-peninsula, with access that is both limited and elusive. At the heart of this coastal haven lies Duck Lagoon, a magical beachfront where people, boats, and ships constantly come and go. Sunny skies, bright mornings, and rocky structures fill the area with charm and beauty. A perfect destination for a weekend or holiday, Duck Lagoon is one of the many places that make Rupestrine exceptional and special. Its surrounding waters are as sweet as candy, leaving a lasting impression on every foreigner. One of the many myths that captivate the minds of the city’s inhabitants is that of a mysterious figure—the ''bride''—who appears every full moon night. Some say she’s a bride, others claim she’s a duck, and some even describe her as a ''duck bride,'' whatever that means. No one knows for sure what she is or if she even exists, but those who have encountered her can assure you that she does. The name of the beach dates back to its founding when Spanish conquistadors took shelter there and accidentally let some of their ducks escape. The birds adapted well to the region and populated it, turning it into the cheerful, duck-filled shore we know today.
