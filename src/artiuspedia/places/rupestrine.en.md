Rupestrine is a city renowned for its rich artistic history. Officially named Rupestrine Union, the city earned its title thanks to the discovery of some of the oldest archaeological sites in the region, filled with cave paintings, petroglyphs, and other forms of rock art. Over time, this deep artistic heritage transformed the city into a cultural hub, attracting musicians, painters, sculptors, dancers, and all those involved in the fine arts. This artistic flourishing culminated in the founding of the EdAN Art University, which stands for Excellens Doctrina Artis Nationum—meaning 'Excellence in the Teaching of the Arts of Nations'—a symbol of the city’s commitment to nurturing creativity. The city is undeniably rich, but many feel that the power is overly concentrated in the hands of the artistic elite. This concentration of influence has led to a sense of political isolation among the general population, fueling movements and growing anti-artist sentiment. The divide between the artists and the people has become a source of tension, with many citizens feeling excluded from the decisions and opportunities that shape their city’s future. While its cultural landscape is vibrant, Rupestrine's economy remains predominantly agricultural, with vast hectares dedicated to the cultivation of beans and coffee, supporting the livelihood of many in the region.
